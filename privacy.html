Privacy Policy

Our commitment to Zero-Cookie security.

Local-First Processing

VetureXtension operates on a strict zero-cookie architecture. We do not collect, store, or transmit your session cookies, personal data, or scraped data to any external servers. All processing happens 100% locally within your active browser tab.

Data Ownership

Any CSV files or data extracted using our utilities remain entirely on your local machine. We cannot see, access, or sell your data.

Payment Processing

Purchases are securely handled by Gumroad. We do not store your credit card information or direct billing details on our servers.
